DITA FLIGHT.006 7806 Sunglasses Buyers Guide: Fake vs Real

Use this checklist to spot real DITA FLIGHT.006 frames and avoid common replica telltales.

What to check

  • Serial markings inside the temple should match DITA format.
  • Frame material should feel dense, not lightweight plastic.
  • Retail packaging should include branded box and cloth.
  • Buy from authorized retailers with return protection.

Common warning signs

  1. Price far below typical retail range.
  2. Missing serial details or inconsistent engraving.
  3. Poor lens finish or uneven tint.
  4. No return policy or vague seller history.

How to verify

  1. Compare serial format with official DITA documentation.
  2. Request additional photos of temples, hinges, and box.
  3. Prefer sellers with transparent policies and traceable shipping.
