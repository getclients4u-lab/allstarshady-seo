Support

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about shipping, returns, exchanges, and checkout.

Last updated: August 2026

Where do you ship?

We currently ship to addresses within the United States.

How long does order processing take?

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday, after payment confirmation.

What is the estimated delivery time?

Once shipped, delivery generally takes 3–5 business days. Total estimated delivery time is 4–7 business days.

Is shipping free?

Yes. We offer free shipping on eligible orders delivered within the United States.

What time is the daily order cut-off?

Our daily order cut-off time is 03:00 PM EST. Orders placed after the cut-off time, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed on the next business day.

Can I return my order?

Yes. We accept returns for both defective and non-defective products within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is new and unused.

Do you accept exchanges?

Yes. Eligible products can be exchanged within 30 days after delivery.

Do I have to pay for return shipping?

No. For approved returns, we provide a free downloadable and printable return shipping label.

Are there any restocking fees?

No. We do not charge restocking fees for approved returns.

When will I receive my refund?

Refunds are typically processed within 7 business days after the returned item is received and approved.

How can I track my order?

Once shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information.

What should I do if my package arrives damaged or I receive the wrong item?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and clear photos of the product and packaging.

Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?

If your order has not yet shipped, contact us immediately and we will update it before dispatch if possible.

Is shopping on your website secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard security measures to help protect personal information and checkout data.

How can I contact Customer Support?

Email support@allstarshady.com or call +1 503 213 9578 Monday–Friday, 09:00 AM–05:00 PM EST.

Still have questions?

Our support team can help with orders, returns, and product questions.
