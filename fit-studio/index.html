Fit FAQs

What's the fastest way to measure my face?

Hold a standard credit card (85.6mm wide) flat under your eyes. If your face spans about one and a half cards you're roughly 128mm; one and three-quarter cards ≈ 150mm; two cards ≈ 171mm. For a precise number, use a ruler or a string + ruler from temple to temple.

What do the numbers on sunglasses mean?

They read as lens-bridge-temple, e.g. 52-18-140. Lens width is the widest point of one lens (48–58 is typical). Bridge is the gap over your nose (14–24). Temple is the arm length (135–150). Total frame width ≈ lens × 2 + bridge.

How do I know if a frame is too narrow?

Frames that are too narrow pinch at the temples, leave marks, or slide down your nose when you smile. As a rule, total frame width should be within ~2–6mm of your temple-to-temple face width — slightly wider looks best.

What if I'm between sizes?

Size up for comfort: a frame a couple mm wider than your face is more forgiving than one that's too narrow. Adjustable nose pads (metal frames) buy you extra flexibility; acetate frames are more rigid, so nail the width.
