Definition list

UV400
Blocks UVA and UVB rays up to 400 nanometers; should be standard for any sunglasses purchase.
Polarized
Lens treatment that reduces glare from horizontal surfaces like roads, water, and sand.
Gradient
Tinted lens that is darker on top and lighter on bottom; useful for driving and reading while shaded.
