Polarized vs Non-Polarized

☀️ Polarized

Blocks horizontal glare from water, roads, and snow. Colors are slightly deeper, and detail pops in bright conditions. Great for driving, fishing, and skiing.

🔘 Non-Polarized

Standard UV protection without glare filtering. Colors remain true to life, and lenses are available in every tint. Good for everyday casual wear and indoor-to-outdoor transitions.
